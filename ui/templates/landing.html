Five stages on a line, a sealed envelope travelling between them, a highlight ring that walks along with it, and five captions in the document that fade with the same keyframes at the same offsets. Every animated property is `opacity` or `transform`, so the browser can composite the whole loop without a layout pass. THE CAPTIONS ARE REAL TEXT IN THE DOCUMENT, not glyphs in the drawing. The SVG carries `role="img"` with a title and a description, so assistive technology reads one sentence about the picture instead of walking a drawing.
