# Design Decisions (ADR Index)

One line per ADR; full records in [adr/](adr/).

- [ADR-001](adr/ADR-001-two-plane-architecture.md) - Two-plane architecture: probabilistic evidence, deterministic decisions. (Accepted 2026-08-10)
- [ADR-002](adr/ADR-002-identity-vault.md) - Sealed identity vault, randomized placeholders, render-time re-hydration. (Accepted 2026-08-10)
- [ADR-003](adr/ADR-003-drafting-after-decision.md) - Drafting after the tier decision, always human-confirmed. (Accepted 2026-08-10; rationale corrected 2026-08-11: par. 31a SGB X is self-authorizing)
- [ADR-004](adr/ADR-004-one-way-valve.md) - One-way valve: anomaly evidence exists only in downgrade conditions, schema-enforced and property-tested. (Accepted 2026-08-10)
- [ADR-005](adr/ADR-005-notifications-as-journal-projections.md) - Applicant notifications as automated informational journal projections. (Accepted 2026-08-10)
- [ADR-006](adr/ADR-006-license-eupl-1-2.md) - License: EUPL-1.2 - copyleft coherent with the inspectability claim, legally binding German text, openCode-aligned; inbound permissive deps unproblematic; LICENSE file and metadata land in part 11. (Accepted 2026-08-12)
- [ADR-007](adr/ADR-007-declarative-predicate-ast.md) - One declarative predicate AST for routing rules and clear-cut criteria; clear-cut is evidence, not a decision-plane computation. (Accepted 2026-08-10)
- [ADR-008](adr/ADR-008-journal-store-protocol.md) - Journal store protocol with sequence-based append-only enforcement; in-memory and JSONL backends until PostgreSQL lands. (Accepted 2026-08-10)
- [ADR-009](adr/ADR-009-procedure-config-composition.md) - One editable home per config value; the loader composes contract shapes. (Accepted 2026-08-10)
- [ADR-010](adr/ADR-010-gold-set-freeze-and-versioning.md) - Gold sets are frozen, versioned and never trained on; corrections supersede a set, never edit it. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-011](adr/ADR-011-declared-divergences.md) - Gold labels state the fachlich correct outcome; known divergences are declared, enforced and still counted. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-012](adr/ADR-012-optional-llm-paraphrase.md) - The LLM paraphrase pass is optional, provenance-stamped and may only touch free text. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-013](adr/ADR-013-content-based-procedure-derivation.md) - The procedure is derived in the evidence plane from declarative content signals; ambiguity and a contradicted hint resolve to "unknown", never to a guess. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-014](adr/ADR-014-explicit-routing-arbitration.md) - Routing arbitration is an explicit integer priority with a total order; an unresolved conflict is recorded and costs routing confidence. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-015](adr/ADR-015-frozen-sets-are-verified-by-integrity.md) - A superseded gold set is verified by its bytes against its own manifest; supersession is recorded in a registry outside the frozen directories. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-016](adr/ADR-016-contracts-batch-research-pass.md) - Contracts batch from the research pass: rule priority, derivation/conflicts on EvidenceRecord, audit_sample_rate, review_due, scorer feature-set exclusions. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-017](adr/ADR-017-seal-at-ingest-transient-witness.md) - Seal identity at ingest, before the envelope exists; a transient request-scoped witness lets the deterministic plane keep validating real values without dereferencing the vault; value-visibility for problem texts, two detector profiles, auto-seal once then refuse. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-018](adr/ADR-018-vault-store-protocol.md) - Vault store protocol with append-only enforcement and two dev backends; fetch is render-time only; the file backend is plaintext and says so, because a home-rolled cipher would look like protection and provide none. (Accepted 2026-08-11)
- [ADR-019](adr/ADR-019-text-layer-over-redacted-text.md) - The text layer is built over the REDACTED text: every span lives in redacted coordinates, the gate seals prose with the deterministic union alone (the corpus asserts that is enough), a placeholder in prose is a correct extraction, and the sweep ignores hits on a placeholder. (Accepted 2026-08-12)
- [ADR-020](adr/ADR-020-two-extractors-one-verifier.md) - Two extractors, one verifier that cannot tell them apart; the double lock checks quote and offset independently and a disagreement is a discard, never a repair; the text.* namespace with two engine-level operators and its config lint; live-model numbers are measured and never gated. (Accepted 2026-08-12, closes P-8)
- [ADR-021](adr/ADR-021-zero-shot-classifier-log-only.md) - The fallback unit classifier is zero-shot similarity against the taxonomy an agency edits, consulted only when no rule fired; log-only is enforced by the decision plane's admitted routing sources rather than by hiding the suggestion; a raw cosine is not a confidence and the loader refuses enablement without a calibration and its provenance; the model is optional and never auto-loaded. (Accepted 2026-08-12)
- [ADR-022](adr/ADR-022-notification-dispatch-ordering.md) - A notification is delivered before it is journaled, and the delivery guarantee is at-least-once deduplicated by an id that is a pure function of the source event and the template; an audit trail that claims a message the citizen never got is worse than one that is briefly incomplete. (Accepted 2026-08-12)
- [ADR-023](adr/ADR-023-rehydration-at-render-time.md) - Re-hydration happens at render time in one module that is the only caller of VaultStore.fetch; an unknown, malformed or unresolvable token blocks the whole draft rather than producing a partial one; the round trip is compared against the raw as-received value; drafts are PII-bearing by design and join the vault on the canary exception list; tier 3 gets no draft, and nothing is dispatched. (Accepted 2026-08-12)
- [ADR-024](adr/ADR-024-shadow-scorer-two-readings.md) - The shadow scorer reads an identity-blind vector twice - an IsolationForest for unusual combinations, a tail share for a single value far out - and scores the percentile of the larger reading in a committed reference population; no feature may restate a decision-table qualifying field (the mirror image of the one-way valve); a sealed value is refused rather than masked, which puts two of gold v4's nine labelled anomalies permanently out of the scorer's reach and back with the rule plane; the threshold is tuned on the frozen gold set and says so; every failure degrades to no evidence and a journaled degradation. (Accepted 2026-08-12)
- [ADR-025](adr/ADR-025-sampled-reason-kind.md) - A deterministic audit draw is not a suspicion: ReasonKind.SAMPLED joins the contract so no consumer can render a randomly audited case with the face of a flagged one; the decision-plane wiring is part 10's migration and journal entries written before it keep the old DOWNGRADED + audit_sample shape. (Accepted 2026-08-12, requested by part 09)
- [ADR-026](adr/ADR-026-review-ui-appends-only.md) - The review UI renders the journal and appends to it and does nothing else: queues and metrics are folds with no store of their own, `machine_tier`/`machine_unit_id` never move and an override is an appended fact next to the decision, the routing answer is the ROUTED event (never the evidence's unadmitted suggestions), confirm stamps the dispatch facts and the par. 66 opt-in re-renders the letter rather than claiming a block the post would not carry, escalation is one click without a mandatory justification because it only ever adds oversight, the xdomea-shaped stub carries no letter and no person so the canary exception list stays at two, metrics aggregate at unit level with a five-confirmation floor, roles are a documented demo gating exactly the re-hydration surface, and the BITV posture is a self-check with the external audit named as pilot scope. (Accepted 2026-08-12)
- [ADR-027](adr/ADR-027-demo-posture-and-reset-by-restart.md) - The public demo is a deployment posture, not a mode the engine knows about: env-gated and default OFF with the flag-off path asserted byte-identical, the ingest gate is MIDDLEWARE rather than a route dependency because FastAPI decodes a body before it solves dependencies (a dependency-based gate answers 422 to the one request shape that matters, having read the stranger's submission first), an unset ingest token means CLOSED and the banner says which of the two postures the instance has, the review actions stay enabled because they are the product, and the reset is a restart by construction - the entrypoint re-seeds from frozen gold v4, so a free tier that stops an idle service is resetting the demo on the best possible schedule and an ephemeral filesystem is the right property rather than a limitation. The image is extra-free by design and CI asserts the extras are not importable. (Accepted 2026-08-13)
- [ADR-028](adr/ADR-028-live-extraction-is-a-local-posture.md) - Live extraction is a LOCAL posture selected once at startup (`EINGANGSLOTSE_EXTRACTOR`, plus URL/model overrides so no frozen config is edited) and replay stays the shipped default everywhere; the failure taxonomy splits at startup only - an unrecognized posture and an unresolvable endpoint refuse to boot, while every runtime model failure is a discard toward tier 3 and never an error to a caller, and nothing probes the endpoint at boot. Backed by the P-16 benchmark this ADR records: two 7B Q4 open-weights models on an RTX 5070, both fast, both schema-valid, both defeated by the double lock (0 and 3 verified spans of ~86, against replay's 88/88), the failure concentrated in offset arithmetic; a blind control shows the models changed the tier on 0 of 24 letters, so on this corpus a 7B model is indistinguishable from no extractor at all. The double lock and the frozen prompt do not move to make the number look better (KE-5). Recommendation: replay-only, including for the local showcase. (Accepted 2026-08-13, closes P-16)
- [ADR-029](adr/ADR-029-demo-journey-and-working-copy-in-ram.md) - The guided three-phase journey is PRESENTATION over the real machinery: the intake page hands its submission to the same `run_ingest` the API route calls and presents the deployment's own ingest token server-side, so it is the authorized caller rather than a bypass and a stranger still gets the part-11 403 (an instance with no token has no phase 1, which is the correct default and the page says so). Personas live in a new independently versioned `config/demo/` the loader never reads, are Mustermann-class fictional, carry checksum-valid Versicherungsnummern encoding their own birth dates, and are asserted not to collide with the canary set or the gold corpus. The pipeline view re-derives nothing - `admitted_routing` stays the routing answer, anomaly reasons go through `render_reason` so the citizen and the caseworker read the same sentence, and a sampled case renders as Qualitaetssicherung. ADR-026's working-copy question gets a DEMO-SCOPE answer only: a two-compartment store, `working_copy` built from the Envelope alone and `echo` from the visitor's own request, RAM-only, 30-minute TTL, 64 entries, absent when the flag is off, never a vault read - and the `echo` half is explicitly the part production must not copy. Phase 3 is the existing review UI plus one display-only highlight that cannot reorder a queue; the e-mail tab is labelled as the simulated adapter it is and says out loud that replay has no recording for a letter a visitor just wrote (ADR-028). Reflow at 320 px is closed for the two new pages and stays open for the caseworker UI. (Accepted 2026-08-13)
- [ADR-030](adr/ADR-030-design-system-and-the-tour.md) - One design system in plain CSS, no build chain and no external fetch of any kind: tokens for palette, type scale and spacing in `ui/static/system.css`, shared components applied through the markup that already existed (`main > section` was always what a card wants to be), and the three old sheets reduced to genuine page-specific leftovers. The type stack is the operating system's rather than a vendored face - fewer things a data-protection demonstration needs in order to render is fewer things it can be wrong about - and every palette pair that ships was computed against the WCAG luminance formula before it was used, with the ratios written into the self-check; dark mode was assessed and not shipped, because a second scheme that had not been verified pair by pair is exactly the "probably fine" this project does not do. The redesign CLOSES P-15's caseworker 1.4.10 row by fixing its cause: the shared reflow rules lived in the citizen-only stylesheet, and moving them into the design system wraps every wide table on every page in its own scroll container - flipped to a static-check verdict that still says nobody has read these pages on a phone. `/metrics` and `/inbox` gained the shell the rest of the site has and joined the mechanical accessibility suite, which now covers six pages. The tour `/demo/rundgang` is demo surface like everything else under `/demo`, tells the whole system in six German steps with a short English aside on each, derives nothing (its one lookup is the journal, and the case's unit and tier come from the same `review_state` fold the caseworker UI reads), points at a SEEDED gold case so the seven stages are walkable on an instance that accepts no submissions, works in both intake postures, and prints no case id at all where nothing was seeded. Zero JavaScript added. (Accepted 2026-08-14)
- [ADR-031](adr/ADR-031-two-languages-one-header-one-hero.md) - Two languages resolved on the SERVER and carried by a cookie: `?lang=` sets it and redirects back, the switch is middleware rather than eleven copies of one decision, and nothing about it needs JavaScript. The table is a PAIR per key, so "German exists and English does not" is unrepresentable; `m()` marks the handful of sentences carrying inline markup and ESCAPES what it interpolates, which `|safe` on a formatted string would not. Visitor pages are translated in full; the caseworker screens stay German in both settings with one `lang="en"` line saying why, because half-translated administrative vocabulary is less usable than the German rather than more; message bodies are never translated at all, being legal-text artifacts out of versioned configuration. Part 11's banner becomes a one-line ribbon on the same gate, linking a demo-gated `/hinweise` that carries the full notice verbatim, the reset model, the ingest posture, the licence and the accessibility summary. One sticky header for every page with a menu that is a native `details`/`summary` - no keydown handler, no focus trap, no hand-maintained `aria-expanded`, and the test pins the absence of all four - demo items behind the posture and the source link rendered only when it is CONFIGURED, because a menu item pointing at github.com/OWNER/... is a broken link in the one place a reader looks for the code. The palette is the reference sky blue with the arithmetic in the token names: `--brand` #4db2ec measures 2.36:1 on white and therefore carries no text ever, `--brand-ink` #106393 does (6.51:1), the reserved red splits the same way (#dc0000 for edges, #8f1010 for text), and a test greps every stylesheet so the rule is structural rather than remembered. Desktop-first by BREAKPOINT and never by user agent (80rem container, columns at 48/64/80rem, personas four across), with the 320 px reflow discipline unchanged and the menu panel un-floating below 40rem. The step indicator becomes numbered circles with SVG checkmarks and keeps `aria-current` plus an offscreen sentence per circle. The landing hero is one inline SVG on a 16-second loop whose five captions are REAL TEXT in the document, stacked in one grid cell and driven by the SAME keyframe set at five negative delays so the picture and the sentence cannot drift apart; `prefers-reduced-motion` is answered explicitly because the design system's blanket rule freezes an animation on its last keyframe, which for a caption is `opacity: 0`. The intake form asks Nachname then Vorname and joins them into the one string the envelope always carried (proved against a literal payload, key order included), uses native date inputs, and feeds its selects from the procedure configuration's own `one_of` so an offered option is by construction an accepted one - with a text fallback when a vocabulary resolves empty and an unknown value KEPT rather than silently changed. Personas supersede to v2 because their shape changed; their data did not. (Accepted 2026-08-14)
- [ADR-032](adr/ADR-032-the-visual-system-and-the-scope-of-the-demo-notice.md) - The visual system grows what three restyles never gave it - an elevation ladder, zoned surfaces and a closing band, fluid type with a rem term in every clamp, state badges drawn around their own words, one control shape - with every NEW pair measured before use (worst new text pair 5.17:1, worst new element 3.64:1; the project floors 5.06 and 3.56 unmoved) and the sky-blue family, the zero-JS posture and the system type stack unchanged. Two defects the measuring itself caught before ship: the focus ring at 1.34:1 on the new dark band (white there now, 11.96:1), and a calendar-button opacity rule dimming the focus ring the browser engine paints inside shadow DOM this project cannot restyle - found by walking all 159 tab stops on nine pages and comparing computed outline against computed background, which is now standing 2.4.7 evidence. The demo ribbon renders where it is about: on / and /hinweise only, the full notice one click away and asserted REACHABLE from every page that lost the one-liner; the scope is pinned on BOTH halves (present on two pages, absent elsewhere, exactly two templates include the partial) so it cannot drift back to everywhere. (Accepted 2026-08-15)
- [ADR-033](adr/ADR-033-simulated-attachments-required-what-arrived-one-way-in.md) - Three user decisions on one page. The e-mail chooser is COMMENTED OUT rather than deleted (a Jinja comment renders zero bytes) and `?kanal=email` falls back to the form, so a bookmark from before shows a page and there is no way in from outside; `build_letter_submission` and both channel ids stay, and restoring the tab is an uncomment plus one tuple - with the honest cost stated, that the redaction refusal is no longer reachable from the page because ADR-017's boundary auto-seals a forged placeholder in a form leaf before refusing anything (tier 2, invalid, the witness still carrying the typed string - a better demonstration, now a hint). Required is WHAT THE PERSONA ARRIVED WITH: one expression over the config's own declaration, read off the declared value so a re-render cannot drop the attribute from the field somebody just emptied, blocking done by the browser with zero JavaScript, red drawn by CSS over `:user-invalid` (never `:invalid`, which paints an untouched form red) with `--alarm` on the edge and `--alarm-text` on the words so no new colour pair enters the project, and a PRE-RENDERED sentence per field so the state has words as well as a tone; Bernd Beispielmann's empty Rentenbeginn is the single exception the rule produces rather than per-persona machinery, his card says so and he is marked deprecation-pending. Prepared synthetic documents become REAL attachment parts (`text` + `sourceType`, which is how a PDF travels here once its text layer is read) under names researched against the DRV's own forms - C0031, R0990, R0810, Geburtsurkunde, Rechnungen in Kopie - with V0028 and the Versicherungsverlauf rejected as wrong-way documents; there is no file upload and the fieldset says so, which is the part-10 refusal stated rather than inferred. NO extractionFixture, measured rather than assumed: the payload already fills every field_map field, so ADR-020's precedence makes every text proposal a `duplicate_field` discard before the double lock runs, and one such entry takes a complete persona from tier 1 to tier 3. Arcs re-pinned deliberately and unchanged with every document ticked; the one number that moves is Bernd's anomaly score (0.109 to 0.505 against 0.86) because the scorer reads the item's shape. Personas supersede to v3. (Accepted 2026-08-18)
- [ADR-034](adr/ADR-034-three-phases-three-grounds-one-design-system.md) - The three phases of the demonstration become three GROUNDS, where a ground is a token set on a body class and nothing else: `:root` stays the citizen canvas untouched, `.ground-machine` is a deep blue-slate for the two pages that show the machine's own work, `.ground-casework` is warm amber paper for the caseworker screens. The surface ladder INVERTS rather than being replaced, which is what keeps every part-18 component rendering correctly with no component-level override, and a test asserts that a ground block declares only custom properties so "one design system, not three stylesheets" is checked rather than intended. The fourth element family arrives with the arithmetic in its name - `--amber` #b07700 measures 3.83:1 on white and 3.06:1 at worst, so yellow draws surfaces and edges and the amber-browns carry every letter - held by the same regex that has held the blue, the red and the caution orange since part 16, plus a new guard on the ALIAS DIRECTION, because `--brand-ink: var(--amber)` would put a 3.83:1 yellow on text duty across every caseworker page without breaking any existing rule. Part 15's refusal of dark mode stands and is not contradicted: that refusal was about rigour rather than darkness, and a ground is a property of the page rather than a reader preference. Every pair was computed before it shipped and the arithmetic is now a TEST - three token blocks parsed out of the stylesheet, `var()` chains resolved, every pair recomputed on all three grounds - which moves 1.4.3 from `reviewed` to `automated`, the only verdict change that row has had. Worst text pair 5.36:1 and 4.99:1, worst element 3.75:1 and 3.06:1, focus ring at or above 8.71:1 and 8.25:1 on every surface; the machine ground's worst numbers beat the light ground's own. Two components broke exactly the way part 18's band broke and both were caught by arithmetic: the current step's number inverting to 2.05:1, and the primary button at 2.30:1 against a dark card, fixed by inverting the fill and its ink through one token. The band inverts on both grounds so it still reads as the page's end, the semantic hues keep their meaning and change value only where a light value would disappear, and the theming changes zero words with the flag-off byte identity, the census sentence and the ribbon pin untouched. Confirmed in a browser: 215 tab stops and 5767 text nodes with nothing below its floor, and the citizen ground pixel-identical to HEAD across sixteen shots. (Accepted 2026-08-18)
