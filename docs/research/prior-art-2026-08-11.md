## Area 1: Positive role models - current facts

### Estonia: proactive family benefits
- Since the launch of proactive family benefit services, 99.99% of registered births are checked automatically for benefit eligibility; the Social Insurance Board pulls from multiple registers and sends the parent a pre-filled offer which the parent confirms (TEHIK family benefit e-service: https://www.tehik.ee/en/family-benefit-e-service, e-Estonia: https://e-estonia.com/proactive-services-estonia/, OECD OPSI: https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/proactive-family-benefits/).
- Government target: 10 proactive "invisible", event-based services (birth, school start, unemployment, etc.) by 2025 (https://www.justdigi.ee/en/digital-communications-and-cyber/digital-services/proactive-government-services, https://mkm.ee/en/e-state-and-connectivity/digital-services/proactive-government-services).
- Key observation: even Estonia's flagship "proactive" service is offer-plus-confirmation, not silent granting. The internationally accepted end state is exactly EingangsLotse's tier-1 pattern (prepared act + human/citizen confirmation). This validates the tier-1 default and gives a strong jury line: "the most digital state in Europe still keeps a confirm click."

### Austria: antragslose Familienbeihilfe (ALF)
- Running since 1 May 2015; register-driven (Zentrales Personenstandsregister -> BMI -> Finanzverwaltung), payment normally lands days after birth with no application (https://www.bundeskanzleramt.gv.at/agenda/familie/familienbeihilfe/basisinformation-zur-familienbeihilfe/antragstellung/antraglose-familienbeihilfe-bei-geburt-eines-kindes.html, https://www.bundeskanzleramt.gv.at/familienportal/aktuell/10-jahre-antragslose-familienbeihilfe.html).
- Failure mode worth stealing as a lesson: when register data is incomplete, cases drop out of the automatic path and the fallback queue is slow - the Austrian Ombudsman handled roughly one hundred cases of months-long, existence-threatening waits (https://eno.ombudsman.europa.eu/home/news/archives-2021/maincontent/enonews-845.html). The exception path is the product. An automation project is judged by the latency of its tier-3 queue, not the speed of its tier-1 path.

### Germany: par. 155(4) AO + par. 88(5) AO RMS - the closest legal-architecture cousin
Verified against the statute text (https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__88.html). Par. 88(5) Satz 3 imposes four minimum guarantees on any tax RMS:

1. Zufallsauswahl: a sufficient number of cases must be selected at random for comprehensive review by officials.
2. Flagged (ausgesteuerte) cases must be reviewed by officials.
3. Officials must retain the power to pull any case into full review.
4. The RMS must be regularly reviewed for goal attainment.

Plus Satz 4: risk parameters are kept secret where publication would endanger uniform and lawful taxation.

Practice notes: since the StModG 2016, unremarkable returns are assessed fully automatically without a human ever seeing the case (https://www.stb-web.de/news/article.php/id/7785); a Landesrechnungshof audit criticized risk-class assignment quality in practice (Rechnungshof RLP Jahresbericht 2021 Nr. 4: https://rechnungshof.rlp.de/fileadmin/rechnungshof/Jahresberichte/2021/Jahresbericht_2021_Nr._04_Einteilung_von_Einkommensteuerfaellen_in_Risikoklassen_als_Bestandteil_des_Risikomanagements.pdf).

Design implications (major):
- EingangsLotse implements guarantees 2 (tier 2/3 human handling) and partially 3 - but has no random-sampling channel and no scheduled RMS goal-attainment review. The one legal architecture the concept cites as precedent mandates random audit of the auto-cleared stream precisely because a zero-false-clear claim is unverifiable without it. This is the biggest single gap found in this research (backlog #1, #3 and closing section).
- Do NOT copy Satz 4 secrecy: it survives in tax law but an equivalent secrecy stance was fatal for SyRI under Art. 8 ECHR in the welfare context. The published-config, feature-level-reasons stance is the right side of that line.

### ELFE / ELFEConnect
- Bremen's combined application (birth certificate + Elterngeld + Kindergeld, once-only register retrieval) is live and offered for Nachnutzung via ELFEConnect; the Nationaler Normenkontrollrat ran an economic-efficiency review in 2025; automatic transfer into the most common Fachverfahren is planned for 2026; alignment with NOOTS is explicit (https://www.mitnutzungsportal.bremen.de/onlinedienste/onlinedienste-fuer-behoerden/elfeconnect-26659, https://serviceportal.gemeinsamonline.de/Onlinedienste/Service/Entry/KombiFamGO, https://www.senatspressestelle.bremen.de/pressemitteilungen/bremer-elfe-bekommt-fluegel-bundesrat-beschliesst-einfach-leistungen-fuer-eltern-348257).
- Lesson: ELFE's decade-long grind shows register plumbing is the slow, political part. EingangsLotse deliberately sits downstream of intake channels rather than inside register infrastructure - keep it that way; position as complement to ELFE/NOOTS ("we triage what still arrives as documents"), never as competitor.

## Area 2: Cautionary tales -> mapped to EingangsLotse guards

### Netherlands toeslagenaffaire
What failed, concretely (https://www.lighthousereports.com/investigation/the-algorithm-addiction/, https://www.vice.com/en/article/how-a-discriminatory-algorithm-wrongly-accused-thousands-of-families-of-fraud/, https://taxadmin.ai/taxadminai-toeslagenaffaire/, https://responsibleailabs.ai/ai-watch/dutch-childcare-benefits-scandal-toeslagenaffaire):

1. Risk model used nationality/dual citizenship and foreign-sounding-name adjacent signals as fraud features.
2. Risk scores were treated as verdicts: full benefit suspension and multi-year clawback without meaningful human assessment.
3. No explanation was available to citizens or often even to caseworkers.
4. The FSV blacklist created a feedback loop: being flagged raised future risk scores and bled into other benefit decisions.
5. Reversal burden fell on citizens (prove innocence to recover money).

Roughly 26,000-35,000 families harmed, 1,100+ children in foster care, government resigned January 2021; the Dutch DPA found institutional discrimination.

Mapping to EingangsLotse guards:

| Toeslagen failure | EingangsLotse guard |
|---|---|
| Protected-adjacent features in the scorer | Identity-blind scorer (vault withholds names, addresses, origin markers) |
| Score treated as decision | One-way valve: a score can only ADD human review, never decide or clear |
| No explanation | Feature-level reasons mandatory on every downgrade |
| Deployed at scale untested | Log-only launch with practitioner flag review |
| Punitive false positives | A "false positive" here means extra human reading, not clawback |
| Wrong auto-clearing | False-clear zero gate on the gold set |

Honest gaps not yet guarded (carried into the closing section):
- Identity-blind is not proxy-blind. Procedural features (document language, translation attachments, foreign-issued document types, channel, completeness patterns) can correlate with origin. Nothing in the current design monitors the distribution of anomaly downgrades for proxy skew.
- Discrimination by delay. The design treats false flags as "merely inefficient", but if flags concentrate on a subgroup, systematically slower processing for that subgroup is itself a discrimination harm - toeslagen began as "extra scrutiny", too.
- No feedback-loop guard is stated as a contract property: the FSV lesson demands that prior flags of the same applicant must never be a scorer feature (no per-applicant flag memory).

### SyRI (Rb. Den Haag, 5 Feb 2020, ECLI:NL:RBDHA:2020:865)
The court struck down the welfare-fraud risk-indication system under Art. 8(2) ECHR: insufficient transparency about the risk model, insufficient safeguards, not necessary/proportionate in a democratic society; opacity itself undermines trust (https://www.europeanrights.eu/index.php?lang=eng&funzione=S&op=2&id=6986, https://www.escr-net.org/caselaw/2020/nederlands-juristen-comite-voor-mensenrechten-et-al-v-netherlands-eclinlrbdha20201878/, https://www.jipitec.eu/jipitec/article/download/324/317/1610).
Lesson: publish the logic. EingangsLotse's open config formats, feature-level reasons, and openCode release are exactly the SyRI-compliant posture; resist any pilot-phase pressure to make downgrade conditions confidential "so applicants cannot game them" (the tax-RMS secrecy model does not transfer to benefits).

### Austria AMS-Algorithmus (AMAS)
Job-seeker chance scoring; the DSB banned the national rollout in August 2020 (missing DSFA, unclear legal basis); after 5+ years of litigation the BVwG ultimately annulled the DSB decision, but the data had been deleted and the project is dead (https://orf.at/stories/3178244/, https://netzpolitik.org/2020/oesterreich-ams-datenschutzbehoerde-stoppt-jobcenter-algorithmus/, https://www.sn.at/wirtschaft/oesterreich/ams-rechtsstreit-ams-algorithmus-183901519).
One lesson: a system can win on the legal merits years later and still die of procedure. Do the DSFA and the legal-basis memo BEFORE the pilot, with the DPA and Personalrat inside the tent - EingangsLotse's plan names the Personalrat; it should equally name the DSFA as a pre-pilot deliverable.

### UK Post Office Horizon
Inquiry Vol. 1 published 8 July 2025; ~1,000 wrongful prosecutions built on the presumption that computer output is correct; executives knew or should have known of defects; BCS and Parliament now push to reverse the legal presumption of computer-evidence reliability (https://www.postofficehorizoninquiry.org.uk/, https://www.bcs.org/articles-opinion-and-research/post-office-horizon-scandal-report-volume-one-bcs-response/, https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5901/cmselect/cmbeis/1598/report.html).
One lesson: institutionalize distrust of your own output. Maintain a public known-errors log per release, journal every span-verification failure rate, and design the UI so the caseworker confirms the evidence, not the system's conclusion.

### Australia Robodebt
Royal Commission 2023: income averaging as sole basis for debt raising was unlawful ("crude and cruel"), burden of disproof shifted to citizens, and the flawed method was so entrenched that insiders could not question it; 57 recommendations including publication of algorithmic rules and independent audit (https://robodebt.royalcommission.gov.au/publications/report, https://lsj.com.au/articles/crude-cruel-and-unlawful-robodebt-royal-commission-findings/).
One lesson: verify the evidence foundation of every automated inference and never let an inference create a citizen obligation. EingangsLotse's completeness gaps must always cite an explicit requirement-list entry plus span evidence (or verified absence), never a statistical inference - and the Nachforderung stays human-confirmed.

## Area 3: German intake/triage practice today

- Input-management vendors (EITCO, BCT, bpi solutions, Kodak Alaris, BLU DELTA/Blumatix and similar) automate type-level classification (document type, sender, metadata, deadline detection) with claimed 95-99% accuracy, plus handover into e-Akte/DMS/Fachverfahren (https://www.eitco.de/blog/digitale-prozesse/automatische-dokumentenklassifikation-in-behoerden-ki-im-digitalen-posteingang/, https://www.bludelta.de/de/dokumentenklassifizierung-sofort-automatisch/). Content-level triage - completeness verdicts, tier logic, anomaly downgrades with legal guard architecture - is not the standard offering. This confirms the whitespace thesis, but note: a bare ">=90% routing accuracy" headline will underwhelm against vendor classification claims; the demo must foreground completeness recall, false-clear gating, and the guard architecture as the differentiators.
- TR-RESISCAN (BSI TR-03138): compliance burden is heavily organizational; Osnabrueck spent >50% of project effort on the Schutzbedarfsanalyse; Thueringen published a simplification guide (https://www.bsi.bund.de/DE/Themen/Oeffentliche-Verwaltung/Moderner-Staat/Ersetzendes-ScannenTR-Resiscan/ersetzendes-scannentr-resiscan_node.html, https://verwaltung.thueringen.de/documents/20142/2394798/Die+Entzauberung+der+TR-Resiscan.pdf, KGSt Leitlinie). Consuming scan output downstream (current plan) is correct; never take custody of scan-compliance obligations.
- FIT-Connect: operational with a public Anbindungskatalog of subscribed authorities; subscribers commit to retrieving submissions within 72h; undelivered submissions get a "reject" event after 14 days (https://docs.fitko.de/fit-connect/docs/faq/, https://docs.fitko.de/fit-connect/docs/organisation-tasks/Anbindungskatalog/). Adoption real but partial (exact authority count unverified). Implication: the FIT-Connect adapter must speak the event log (accept/reject semantics), not just receive payloads - the instant receipt should be implemented as the protocol-conformant acknowledgement.
- BAMF as deployed-AI precedent: dialect recognition, Arabic transliteration, and document data extraction run in the asylum process; documented problems: unbalanced training data (6,680 Levantine vs 1,158 Iraqi Arabic samples), scientific validation missing for 5+ years after rollout, and "only a hint" outputs de facto steering decisions (https://algorithmwatch.org/de/dialekterkennung-bamf/, https://netzpolitik.org/2022/asylverfahren-bamf-weitet-automatische-sprachanalyse-aus/, https://www.bpb.de/themen/migration-integration/kurzdossiers/migration-und-sicherheit/570377/kuenstliche-intelligenz-im-migrationsmanagement/). Lesson: "assistive only" is not a guard; automation bias makes hints into decisions unless the architecture prevents it. The one-way valve is the structural version of what BAMF only promised.
- Bundesagentur fuer Arbeit: every second ALG application arrives electronically and is processed and approved automatically (BA statement, 2024); Kindergeld is end-to-end digital with BundID (https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/freiberg/presse/2024-34-mit-der-bundid-antrage-online-stellen, https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/familie-und-kinder/eservices-fuer-familien). Precedent that mass-procedure automation at a federal target agency is politically accepted.
- DRV: "rvEvolution" confirmed as the cross-carrier multi-project replacing the core system with "rvSystem:modern" (target ~2026, likely slipping - uncertainty), including a "KI-Boost@rvEvolution" maximum-automation ambition (https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/Bund/DE/Ueber-uns/Digitalstrategie/digitalstrategie_node.html, https://www.bundesamtsozialesicherung.de/de/themen/digitalausschuss/digitalisierung-in-der-gesetzlichen-rentenversicherung/). A DRV pilot pitch should present EingangsLotse as a bolt-on to the rvEvolution seam, not a parallel system.
- Verwaltungs-LLMs (all verified to exist): F13 Baden-Wuerttemberg - prototype since May 2023, product 2024, open source since 23 July 2025 (https://stm.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/service/presse/pressemitteilung/pid/ki-assistenz-f13-wird-zur-open-source-software). LLMoin Hamburg - pilot ~100 users, production since Dec 2024, ~40,000 users, >80% satisfaction Oct 2025, reused by four Laender (https://www.dataport.de/nachricht/kuenstliche-intelligenz-hamburg-startet-mit-ki-assistent-llmoin-in-den-regelbetrieb/, https://www.hamburg.de/politik-und-verwaltung/behoerden/senatskanzlei/aktuelles/pressemeldungen/hamburg-etabliert-ki-assistenten-llmoin-als-bundesweit-nutzbares-erfolgsmodell-1127988). Parla Berlin - CityLab RAG search over parliamentary documents, open source (https://www.technologiestiftung-berlin.de/projekte/parla). Also: the BVA hosts the federal Beratungszentrum fuer KI (BeKI) (https://www.digitale-verwaltung.de/Webs/DV/DE/transformation/zentraler-eingangskanal/zentraler-eingangskanal.html).
- Net finding: every deployed German Verwaltungs-LLM is a general assistant or RAG search. No published German project does guarded inbound triage with tier decisions. The lane is empty, and the acceptance evidence (LLMoin's 80% satisfaction) shows caseworkers adopt AI tools that assist rather than judge.

## Area 4: Open-source / component landscape

- openCode.de: no direct intake-triage competitor surfaced in search (uncertainty: openCode's GitLab is not fully indexed by web search engines; do a direct gitlab.opencode.de search before submission and cite the absence). F13 lives there as reusable admin-AI infrastructure; "Document Writing Tools" (federal CIO) covers outbound document automation, not intake (https://opencode.de/de, https://gitlab.opencode.de/open-code/document-writing-tools).
- OpenRedact (BMBF Prototype Fund; https://gitlab.opencode.de/bmbf/datenlabor/openredact): semi-automatic German document anonymization; its NERwhal component unions multiple recognizers (spaCy, flair, regex) with scoring, precisely because single German NER models are weak (https://medium.com/@openredact/nerwhal-a-multi-lingual-suite-for-ner-d3ac6beb547). Prototype status, not maintained - reuse the multi-recognizer-union design idea and its German recognizer patterns, not the code. Its semi-automatic stance (human reviews low-confidence redactions) is a useful precedent for the canary pass.
- Teuken-7B / OpenGPT-X: project ended 31 March 2025; model open on Hugging Face, offered commercially by T-Systems and IONOS - but no successor training is funded, so it is aging (https://www.iais.fraunhofer.de/en/industries_and_cross-sector_solutions/cross-sector_solutions/generative-ai/opengpt-x.html, https://www.telekom.com/en/media/media-information/archive/opengpt-x-language-model-made-in-germany-1084484). The docs' "Mistral- or OpenGPT-X-class" phrasing is right; the sovereignty story should rest on swap-by-config, with Teuken as the demonstration of swappability rather than the primary model.

## Area 5: Best practice for the next build parts

### (a) Redaction / pseudonymization (part 04)
- Presidio is probabilistic; Microsoft explicitly does not guarantee recall; defaults are English-tuned and recall drops sharply without a properly configured German model; missing custom recognizers is "the number-one reason real PII slips through"; golden-file recall testing before production is the accepted practice (https://microsoft.github.io/presidio/analyzer/adding_recognizers/, https://explainx.ai/blog/microsoft-presidio-pii-detection-anonymization-guide-2026).
- Concrete: build German-specific recognizers (Steuer-ID with checksum, Rentenversicherungsnummer format, IBAN, Aktenzeichen patterns, German address grammar), keep the second-detector sweep, and - the real gap - measure redactor recall itself as a CI metric on a seeded PII golden set, separate from the canary assertions. Treat output as pseudonymized (GDPR still applies), never claim anonymization.

### (b) Span-grounded extraction (part 05)
Current research converges on exactly the planned architecture: grounding every extracted element to a source span makes hallucination detectable and eliminable (https://www.mdpi.com/2073-431X/15/3/178); span-level hallucination detection over structured documents is an active benchmark area (https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00895v1); the "double-lock" pattern requires the model to emit a verbatim grounding quote plus the value, verified independently. Refinement: require quote AND offset and verify both against the normalized text layer - offset alone can drift on OCR text, quote alone can be found at the wrong location.

### (c) Calibration and threshold governance (parts 06, 09, config)
- UK Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard (ATRS): mandatory template (2024) recording purpose, human involvement, review/challenge paths for public-sector algorithmic tools. Publishing an ATRS-style record per config version is cheap and jury-visible.
- UK DWP cautionary detail: its UC risk model was fairness-assessed against only 2 of 9 protected characteristics for lack of data (https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/politicsandpolicy/the-uks-ai-governance-gap/) - state explicitly which fairness checks are and are not possible on synthetic data.
- Par. 88(5) Nr. 4 AO's periodic goal-attainment review is the German statutory template: put a review_due / review-protocol field into AgencyRiskConfig so threshold governance is a config artifact, not a promise.
- "Meaningful human involvement" is the legal hinge for Art. 22: monitor unit-level (never per-person) confirm-without-edit rates and median time-to-confirm as rubber-stamp indicators.

### (d) BITV 2.0 for internal tools (part 10)
BITV 2.0 no longer distinguishes public from non-public: it covers intranets, extranets, internally used apps, and electronic administrative workflows; conformity is presumed when EN 301 549 V3.2.1 is met, which binds WCAG 2.1 A + AA (https://www.bundesfachstelle-barrierefreiheit.de/DE/Fachwissen/Informationstechnik/EU-Webseitenrichtlinie/BGG-und-BITV-2-0/Die-neue-BITV-2-0/die-neue-bitv-2-0_node). The caseworker UI is legally in scope, not voluntarily so - test against EN 301 549 V3.2.1 / WCAG 2.1 AA (keyboard-only queue handling and screen-reader-usable evidence spans are the hard cases).

## The 3 things most likely wrong or missing, given the prior art

1. **No random audit of the cleared stream.** The architecture's own legal role model, par. 88(5) AO, makes Zufallsauswahl of auto-processed cases a statutory minimum - because a "false-clear rate: zero" claim is epistemically empty once no cleared case is ever re-examined. The gold-set zero gate proves the config on frozen data; only random sampling proves it on live intake. Small contract change now (backlog #1), architecture rework later.

2. **The fairness story stops at identity-blindness.** Toeslagen's deepest failure was not that the model saw nationality - it is that nobody watched where the flags landed. Procedural features can proxy for origin (document language, translations, foreign document types), and "false flags are merely inefficient" ignores that concentrated delay is itself a harm. Currently no downgrade-distribution monitoring, no proxy-skew alarm, no contract clause banning per-applicant flag history from the feature set.

3. **One-click confirm can decay into a rubber stamp, and nothing currently measures that.** BAMF's "only a hint" systems steer decisions in practice; Horizon shows institutions default to trusting system output. Art. 22 protection stands or falls on meaningful human involvement, and the journal proves who clicked, not whether they looked. Without unit-level confirm-without-edit and time-to-confirm metrics (and possibly evidence-first UI gating), the strongest legal claim in the concept rests on an unmeasured assumption.

One more honest note: the whitespace claim held up - no German project found (F13, LLMoin, Parla, BeKI, input-management vendors) does guarded content-level intake triage - but openCode's GitLab is poorly indexed by search engines, so verify the absence directly on gitlab.opencode.de before asserting it to a jury.
