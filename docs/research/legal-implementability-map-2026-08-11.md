## 0. Two corrections to the project's prior legal framing (both verified)

1. **Par. 31a SGB X contains no Rechtsvorschrift-Vorbehalt.** Verified text (unchanged since 01.01.2017): "Ein Verwaltungsakt kann vollstaendig durch automatische Einrichtungen erlassen werden, sofern kein Anlass besteht, den Einzelfall durch Amtstraeger zu bearbeiten. Setzt die Behoerde automatische Einrichtungen zum Erlass von Verwaltungsakten ein, muss sie fuer den Einzelfall bedeutsame tatsaechliche Angaben des Beteiligten beruecksichtigen, die im automatischen Verfahren nicht ermittelt wuerden." This is the par. 155(4) AO model, self-authorizing. Only par. 35a VwVfG (not applicable to DRV; SGB X displaces it) has the "durch Rechtsvorschrift zugelassen ... weder Ermessen noch Beurteilungsspielraum" wording. Consequence: for DRV the question is never "does an authorizing provision exist" (par. 31a itself is one); it is whether the norm's substantive limits are met (Ermessens-/Beurteilungsentscheidungen always give "Anlass" for human processing; Art. 22(2)(b) DSGVO safeguards required). The conservative posture stays right; the stated reason needed rewording (errata C-11, applied: ADR-003 correction note, EM/AR config headers via part 03b).
2. **AI Act high-risk deadline moved.** The Digital Omnibus amending Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 was published in the OJ 24 July 2026, in force 27 July 2026 [verified via secondary legal commentary; verify against the OJ text before external citation]. Annex III standalone high-risk obligations (incl. Art. 26, 27) now apply from 2 December 2027, not 2 August 2026. Still on the original schedule: Art. 5 prohibitions, Art. 4 AI literacy (Feb 2025), GPAI rules (Aug 2025), Art. 50 transparency (2 Aug 2026). Build as if Annex III applied: a pilot outlives the deferral. NOTE: the concept (canonical, user-owned) states the old date; flagged to the user rather than edited.

Also verified: par. 37 Abs. 2 SGB X Bekanntgabefiktion is now FOUR days (post and electronic; PostModG 2025) - part-08 deadline math must use 4 days. Par. 9 Abs. 2 SGB VI now reads "sind zu erbringen" (bound "ob"); the Auswahlermessen of par. 13 Abs. 1 SGB VI remains.

## 2. Framework detail

- **Par. 31a SGB X / 35a VwVfG / 155(4) AO:** three siblings of the 2016 modernization; AO and SGB X self-authorizing with a "kein Anlass" risk filter; VwVfG requires a specific Rechtsvorschrift. Par. 88 Abs. 5 AO RMS (random sampling, risk rules, mandatory human referral on free text) is the operational template for "kein Anlass". Par. 31a S. 2 hard-codes: individually significant submissions automation would not detect MUST be considered - any unstructured content must force human processing. DRV's GRA on par. 31a exists but was not retrievable [verify for pilot file].
- **Art. 22 DSGVO:** receipt/status Realakte are not decisions - out of Art. 22 entirely. CJEU C-634/21 (SCHUFA): an automated preparatory step is itself the decision if the human step is a rubber stamp. Tier-1 one-click confirm is lawful only if review is demonstrably meaningful: evidence shown, edit/reject as easy as confirm, override rates monitored. The journal is the proof instrument; make the rubber-stamp rebuttal a measured artifact.
- **AI Act:** the deterministic rule engine is likely not an "AI system" (Art. 3(1), Recital 12: rules defined solely by humans). The shadow scorer IS an AI system; Annex III 5(a) not squarely met (cannot grant/reduce/revoke - one-way valve); Art. 6(3)(c)/(d) derogation fits triage; BORDERLINE via the profiling subparagraph (Art. 6(3) subpara. 2, Art. 4(4) GDPR). Verdict: defensible as out of high-risk scope, not safely out of scope of argument. Prudent: documented Art. 6(4)-style assessment, Art. 49(2) registration if derogation claimed, voluntarily meet Art. 26 deployer duties (oversight by trained persons, >= 6 months log retention, Art. 26(7) worker-representative information) and Art. 27 FRIA. Legally bites 2 Dec 2027; FRIA and worker information should precede any pilot regardless (BPersVG, DSGVO apply now).
- **Mitwirkungspflichten (par. 60-67 SGB I):** a lawful, deadline-effective Nachforderung contains (1) precise identification of each gap, (2) the par. 60 SGB I anchor, (3) a concrete calendar deadline, (4) the channel, and - only if par. 66 consequences are reserved - (5) a case-specific written Rechtsfolgenhinweis (BSG: konkret and unmissverstaendlich, no boilerplate). The Versagung itself is an Ermessens-VA - permanently human. Amtsermittlung guard: no par. 66 threat for facts DRV can determine itself (missing VSNR is usually determinable from the Konto - request, do not threaten). Schriftform: FIT-Connect status or plain email does NOT satisfy par. 66 Abs. 3 "schriftlich" (par. 36a SGB I qualified means needed). Bekanntgabe fiction: 4 days.

## 3. Per-procedure notes

- **Altersrente:** par. 35 SGB VI fully gebunden - the one procedure where par. 31a full automation is doctrinally reachable [analysis]. Blockers today are substantive (see table). tier1_enabled: true confirmed; clear_cut restricted to Regelaltersrente ohne Auslandsbezug is the right legal line. Flip-package requirements listed as C-12.
- **Erwerbsminderungsrente:** formally gebundener Anspruch, but the tatbestandliche assessment is an evaluative sozialmedizinische Beurteilung with prognosis - full automation off the table permanently. The config comment "once a legal basis under par. 31a SGB X exists" is wrong in direction (the basis exists; the facts can never satisfy it) - errata via 03b. The em-0001 complete-but-tier-3 shape is exactly right.
- **Rehabilitation:** grant content discretionary (par. 13 Abs. 1 SGB VI); no tier-1 grant preparation ever. Par. 14 SGB IX clocks (2 weeks Zustaendigkeit/forward, 3 weeks decision) make automated instant routing legally valuable - surface the clock in the queue (part 10).
- **Widerspruch:** triage may acknowledge (Realakt), fix Eingangszeitpunkt (evidentially precious, par. 84 SGG), extract the contested Aktenzeichen, route to the Widerspruchsstelle. Must not auto-assess Fristwahrung/admissibility or imply merits. The missing routing rule (xx-0005) is a product decision, not a legal barrier; deadline capture should not wait for the full Widerspruch workflow.
- **Unknown items:** auto-routing to a clearing unit is an interne Organisationsentscheidung, Realakt, unconstrained; par. 16 Abs. 2 SGB I Weiterleitungspflicht and par. 14 SGB IX apply; par. 17 SGB I zuegige Leistungserbringung generally.
